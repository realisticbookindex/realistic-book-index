# The filter card

**Fill this in before running Rule 13. It is section 3 of that prompt.**

Rule 13 section 3 prints with three blanks. They are blanks on purpose: the practitioner filter is
one player's observation, and a replicator's card is a different player's. This is the step of the
method that cannot be inherited, and it is the reason a replication is worth running at all.

The instrument treats whatever you write here as the governing evidence on call likelihood. It
can exclude a title from Lists 1, 2 and 3 on this card alone. **A run with the brackets left in
is not a run**, because ground 4 of Stage One then has no setting to test against and the prompt
instructs the model to treat it as unverified corpus.

Replace each bracket, delete this heading and the notes, and paste the table into Rule 13
section 3.

---

```
  settings      instrumental jam sessions, professional engagements, and vocalist-led work in
                active metropolitan scenes, including academic settings observed from the
                bandstand

                [Edit this line if your settings differ. If you do not play vocalist-led work,
                say so - the instrument will otherwise exclude on a setting you cannot observe.]

  scene         principally [YOUR GEOGRAPHICAL SCENE]

                [Name the city or region. A card reading "the United States" excludes nothing,
                because no one observes it.]

  period        observation from [YEAR] to present

                [The year you first encountered live jazz sessions. This is what makes the card
                datable by a reader.]

  instrument    [YOUR PRIMARY INSTRUMENT OR INSTRUMENTS]
  and vantage

                [Vantage matters as much as instrument. A player who calls tunes and a player
                who is called are observing two different things.]

  not covered   traditional and swing-era settings, assessed separately under List 4, where
                survey convergence is expressly not the governing test

                [Name every setting your observation does not reach. This line is what keeps the
                instrument from excluding on evidence you do not have.]
```

## What this edition's card says

Printed at Draft 10.7 and reproduced here so that a replicator has a worked example rather than
an empty form:

- **settings** - instrumental jam sessions, professional engagements, and vocalist-led work
- **scene** - principally South Florida, with periods in New York and Boston
- **period** - observation from 1988 to present
- **instrument and vantage** - guitar, from the bandstand
- **not covered** - traditional and swing-era settings

**Do not copy it.** A replication that adopts this card has replicated the arithmetic and not the
method, and the volume says so at its close: *a replicator applying the printed declaration is
applying a description of an observation rather than the observation.* A run that lands on this
edition's total of 729 has adopted this volume's declarations rather than made its own.
